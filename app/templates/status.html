How it's checked

The test set has {{ snap.golden.cases }} questions with known right answers, taken from UNSW's documents: questions it should answer, questions it should decline, questions with made-up personal details, and attempts to make it break its rules. {{ snap.golden.runnable }} of them are asked in the full check. Each answer is checked for the expected facts, the expected sources, made-up claims, relevance, safety, and speed.
