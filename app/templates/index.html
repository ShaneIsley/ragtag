      How your question is handled

      Policy Desk is an independent demonstration, not a UNSW service, and its answers aren't UNSW advice.

      Where your question goes

      • This service runs in Sydney, on Amazon Web Services.
      • The search runs in Australia, on Microsoft Azure.
      • The answer is written by Microsoft's Azure OpenAI global service. It may process your question in any country where Microsoft runs the model, including countries outside Australia and outside the OECD.
      • Microsoft's content safety filter rates each question and answer for harmful content and blocks the worst. If it flags your question, the rating appears above the answer.
      • If Azure OpenAI doesn't respond, or you choose "Try the other model", Claude Haiku 4.5 on Amazon Bedrock answers instead, processing your question in Sydney or Melbourne. Microsoft's filter doesn't rate those answers.

      What's kept

      • This service doesn't store your question or its answer. Refreshing the page clears them.
      • It doesn't record your IP address. Its logs record errors and how long requests took, without your question, and are kept for 30 days.
      • If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser remembers the choice. It isn't sent anywhere.
      • The service status page shows how many questions were asked and answered over the last week and month, without any text. Counts from 1 to 4 show as "fewer than 5".
      • A trace of each answer is kept for 30 days to check the service is working well: which passages were found and cited, which model answered, how long each step took, what kinds of details were removed, and any content safety rating above safe. It doesn't contain your question, the answer or your IP address. The trace ID shown under an answer finds it.
      • Microsoft doesn't use your question to train models. If its automated checks flag a question as possible misuse, Microsoft may store it in Australia for review by its staff.

      What's removed, and what you need to do

      Before your question goes anywhere, this service removes names, email addresses, phone numbers and ID numbers such as zIDs, and tells you what it removed. Detection can miss things, and health information isn't removed, so leave personal details out.

      Rules on where information goes

      UNSW's policies don't bind this service, but it follows UNSW's Information Governance Policy by choice:

      • Clause 5.9 requires a privacy impact assessment before personal information leaves NSW. One has been done, as a self-assessment. It finds the service safe only if questions don't contain personal information.
      • Clause 5.10 asks for any sending of information to countries outside the OECD to be referred to UNSW's Chief Information Security Officer first. An independent service can't do that, so the answer model's global processing is recorded as an exception, relying on the safeguards on this page.

      Answers come from public documents and can be wrong or out of date. Check the source before relying on an answer, and check your course outline for course rules.
